How leads are chosen and scored
  • Removed automatically: hospital and university systems, government bodies, national chains (labs, pharmacies, dialysis, urgent care), FQHCs, nursing and adult homes, hospital-run clinics, dentists, and addresses where six or more new clinicians register at once (usually a hospital or large group). Tick "Show filtered-out records" to see them.
  • Points for: a brand-new practice entity or lab, a new physician, target specialties from xybermed.com, solo practitioners, private-practice naming (PLLC, PC, Associates), a doctor and a new practice at the same address, and recency.
  • Tiers: Hot 70+, Warm 50 to 69, Watch below 50. Each lead shows its reasons and a suggested angle.
Sources: CMS NPPES weekly files (new NPIs, roughly the last 5 weeks) and the NYS Department of Health facility registry. A new NPI means a provider or entity was registered, which usually precedes opening and payer enrollment, but it is not proof of an open practice. Fit scores are a heuristic built from xybermed.com's services. Respect Do-Not-Call, CAN-SPAM and TCPA rules when reaching out.
